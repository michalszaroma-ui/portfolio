/about

A route is a route, whether it carries a pallet or a packet.

At Saica Pack I plan delivery routes across Ireland, Northern Ireland and the UK. That means choosing paths under changing conditions, keeping records accurate across systems, and spotting the one bad address before it costs a day. Network engineering asks the same questions in a different vocabulary: what path does this take, what happens when it fails, and who is allowed to touch it.

That is what I am studying for now. I hold a 2004 IT Technician diploma from Krakow, I use ERP and warehouse systems daily, and I am currently working toward CompTIA Security+ alongside the degree.
